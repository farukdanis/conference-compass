Privacy

Your goals, preferences and personal plan are processed and stored only in your browser (localStorage). The multilingual AI model is downloaded from Hugging Face on first use and runs locally in your browser; your goal text is never sent to a server.

Optional analytics. When the site operator enables the optional first-party analytics backend, the app sends a small set of allow-listed events (for example “plan completed” or a session ID from the published programme) to the same site. No cookies, visitor IDs, fingerprints, raw IP addresses, full referrers, goals, searches or personal notes are stored. Each dimension is kept as a separate daily total, so visits cannot be reconstructed. Counters older than 90 days are purged. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected. Hosting infrastructure still processes connection metadata to serve pages.

Gizlilik (özet)

Hedeflerin, tercihlerin ve planın yalnızca tarayıcında saklanır. Yapay zekâ modeli ilk kullanımda Hugging Face'ten indirilir ve tarayıcında çalışır; yazdığın hedef sunucuya gönderilmez. Site işletmecisi isteğe bağlı ölçümü açarsa yalnızca çerezsiz, kimliksiz günlük toplamlar tutulur; DNT ve GPC'ye uyulur.
